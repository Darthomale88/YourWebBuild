What's included in the $100 build? ›
A custom, hand-coded website — mobile responsive, with a contact form, basic on-page SEO, SSL, analytics, and Search Console set up. You also get launch support and a walkthrough so you feel confident owning it.
Do I have to pay the $49/mo? ›
No. The care plan is optional. If you'd rather, we hand over all your files and you host the site yourself with no monthly fee. Either way, the site is yours.
What does the $49/mo care plan cover? ›
Hosting, security, SSL, automatic backups, uptime monitoring, minor edits and content updates, software updates, and direct support from me. It's month-to-month with no long-term contract.
What's the Google Business Profile add-on? ›
A one-time $250 service where I set up or optimize your Google Business Profile — the listing that shows up in Google Maps and local search. It's how neighbors find your hours, reviews, and contact info when they search for your kind of business.
Can I cancel the care plan anytime? ›
Yes. It's month-to-month — just let me know before your next billing date. If you cancel, I hand over your files so you can host the site yourself or move it anywhere you like.
Do I own my website? ›
Always. Whether you stay on a care plan or host it yourself, the site is yours to keep.
